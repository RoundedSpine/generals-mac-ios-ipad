# Community Patch integration notices

EA has not endorsed and does not support this product.

The `community-patch-ios` branch integrates the Full English data package from
[The Super Patch for Generals: Zero Hour](https://github.com/RoundedSpine/GeneralsGamePatch/tree/6c7f40d6af6a4babb77a33a4502ec7656446c13e),
by The Super Hackers and its contributors. Original Generals and Zero Hour 1.04
are required; they are not included in the IPA.

The source is provided under its [GPL v3 license](https://github.com/RoundedSpine/GeneralsGamePatch/blob/6c7f40d6af6a4babb77a33a4502ec7656446c13e/LICENSE.txt)
and [Electronic Arts and The Super Hacker terms](https://github.com/RoundedSpine/GeneralsGamePatch/blob/6c7f40d6af6a4babb77a33a4502ec7656446c13e/TERMS.txt).
Individual asset license notices are preserved in the staged patch's `Notices`
directory, alongside `TERMS.txt`, `UPSTREAM-README.md` and its source manifest.
The upstream source and its pinned builder contain the corresponding inputs and
conversion instructions. See [the integration guide](docs/BUILD/IOS_COMMUNITY_PATCH.md).
